# Competitive Analysis Workflow

Paste this into Claude Desktop to run a deep competitive analysis.

---

**Prompt:**

> Run a competitive analysis against: [COMPETITOR NAMES OR FEATURE AREA]
>
> Follow these steps in order:
>
> 1. Read `context/market.md` and `context/product.md`. Check `outputs/strategy/` for existing competitive analysis
> 2. Use `competitive-brief` — identify competitors across 4 types (Direct, Indirect, Adjacent, Substitute). Research product pages, pricing, changelogs, reviews, job postings
> 3. Deep teardown each competitor: company summary, product positioning, recent momentum, feature comparison (5-level scale), pricing model with hidden costs
> 4. Use `obviously-awesome` and `blue-ocean-strategy` — build 2x2 positioning maps, find gaps and blue ocean, identify competitors' deliberate trade-offs
> 5. Strategic response for each market trend: Lead, Fast Follow, Monitor, or Ignore?
> 6. Use `competitive-battlecard` — create per-competitor sales battlecards (their pitch, our counter, proof points, objection handling)
> 7. Save brief to `outputs/strategy/competitive-brief_2026-04-06.md` and battlecards to `outputs/sales/`
> 8. Update `context/market.md`
