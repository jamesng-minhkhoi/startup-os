# Growth Audit Workflow

Paste this into Claude Desktop to audit your growth engine.

---

**Prompt:**

> Run a growth audit for Petio. Here's the current state: [PASTE METRICS OR DESCRIBE WHAT'S WORKING/NOT]
>
> Follow these steps in order:
>
> 1. Read `context/metrics.md` for current KPIs
> 2. Use `metrics-review` and `north-star-metric` — is our NSM right? Review the full AARRR funnel and identify the bottleneck
> 3. Use the appropriate CRO skill for the bottleneck (`signup-flow-cro`, `onboarding-cro`, `paywall-upgrade-cro`, or `page-cro`) — score each conversion point 0-10
> 4. Use `marketing-ideas` and `content-strategy` to evaluate channels — which to double down on, which to cut
> 5. Use `improve-retention` and `hooked-ux` to analyze retention — map the Hook model (Trigger, Action, Variable Reward, Investment)
> 6. Use `growth-loops` to identify flywheel effects and referral mechanisms
> 7. Save to `outputs/marketing/growth-audit_2026-04-06.md` with scored bottleneck analysis and prioritized fixes
> 8. Update `context/metrics.md`
