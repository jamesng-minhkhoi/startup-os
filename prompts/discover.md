# Discovery Workflow

Paste this into Claude Desktop to run the full product discovery cycle.

---

**Prompt:**

> Run a product discovery cycle for: [DESCRIBE WHAT YOU'RE DISCOVERING]
>
> Follow these steps in order:
>
> 1. Read `context/customers.md` and `context/product.md` for existing knowledge
> 2. Use the `customer-research` and `mom-test` skills to analyze what we know and design interview questions that pass The Mom Test
> 3. Use `jobs-to-be-done` to map functional, emotional, and social jobs — identify which are underserved
> 4. Use `identify-assumptions-existing` to list and prioritize critical assumptions by risk and impact
> 5. Use `brainstorm-experiments-existing` and `lean-startup` to design validation experiments for each top assumption — target real commitment, not stated preference
> 6. Score each section 0-10
> 7. Save the synthesis to `outputs/product/discovery_2026-04-06.md`
> 8. Update `context/customers.md` with new insights
