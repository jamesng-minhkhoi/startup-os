# Pitch Builder Workflow

Paste this into Claude Desktop to build an investor pitch narrative.

---

**Prompt:**

> Build an investor pitch for Petio. Stage: [pre-seed/seed/series A]. Ask: [amount]. [ANY SPECIFIC INVESTOR CONCERNS TO ADDRESS]
>
> Follow these steps in order:
>
> 1. Read all context files for the full picture
> 2. Use `storybrand-messaging` and `made-to-stick` — build the pitch arc: Problem > Insight > Solution > Traction > Ask. Customer is the hero. Apply SUCCESs (Simple, Unexpected, Concrete, Credible, Emotional, Story)
> 3. Use `obviously-awesome` — what market category are we claiming, competitive alternatives, one-sentence positioning
> 4. Use `competitive-brief` — TAM/SAM/SOM with bottom-up methodology
> 5. Use `hundred-million-offers` — apply the Value Equation to show why it's a no-brainer
> 6. Use `influence-psychology` — apply social proof, scarcity, anchoring, loss aversion (ethically)
> 7. Generate a 10-12 slide deck outline: Title, Problem, Solution, Market Size, Product/Demo, Business Model, Traction, Competition, Team, Financials, Ask, Appendix
> 8. Save to `outputs/strategy/pitch_2026-04-06.md`
