# Canvas Workshop Workflow

Paste this into Claude Desktop to generate or update a strategic canvas.

---

**Prompt:**

> Generate a [startup/lean/bmc] canvas for Petio. [OR: help me figure out which canvas type I need — here's what I'm trying to understand: ...]
>
> Follow these steps in order:
>
> 1. Read all context files (`context/company.md`, `context/product.md`, `context/market.md`, `context/customers.md`, `context/metrics.md`)
> 2. Use `startup-canvas` (recommended), `lean-canvas`, or `business-model` — walk through each section, challenge weak entries, cross-reference for consistency. For Startup Canvas: apply the Can't/Won't defensibility test
> 3. Use `lean-startup` — for each section identify the riskiest assumption, what evidence do we have, what Validation Ladder level. Design experiments for top 3 unvalidated assumptions
> 4. Use `blue-ocean-strategy` and `crossing-the-chasm` — overlay with market strategy, where on adoption lifecycle, who's the beachhead
> 5. Save to `outputs/strategy/canvas_2026-04-06.md` and update `context/product.md` and `context/market.md`
