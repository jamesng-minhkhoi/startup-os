# Idea Validation Workflow

Paste this into Claude Desktop to validate a startup or product idea.

---

**Prompt:**

> Validate this idea: [DESCRIBE YOUR IDEA — WHAT IT IS, WHO IT'S FOR, WHAT PROBLEM IT SOLVES]
>
> Follow these steps in order:
>
> 1. Push me for specificity if my idea is vague
> 2. Use `mom-test` — score quality of customer conversations 0-10, design 5-7 Mom Test questions that could falsify the idea, identify the scariest question I haven't asked
> 3. Use `startup-canvas` and `lean-startup` — fill in the Startup Canvas with defensibility test (Can't/Won't), identify the riskiest assumption per section, score 0-10
> 4. Use `hundred-million-offers` — apply the Value Equation (Dream Outcome x Perceived Likelihood / Time Delay x Effort), score the offer 0-10
> 5. Use `blue-ocean-strategy` and `crossing-the-chasm` — red vs blue ocean? Where on the adoption lifecycle? Who's the beachhead?
> 6. Give a clear GO / ITERATE / KILL verdict with specific reasoning. No hedging
> 7. Save to `outputs/strategy/validation_2026-04-06.md` and update `context/product.md`
