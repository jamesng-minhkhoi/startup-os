# Market Context

## Market Size
- **TAM**: Global parenting/baby app market — ~$2.8B (2025), growing ~12% CAGR
- **SAM**: English-speaking parents of babies aged 0–12 months using iOS/Android — est. ~$300–500M
- **SOM**: Allergen introduction niche (parents starting solids, ages 4–8 months) — est. ~$5–20M addressable in Year 1
- **Methodology**: Bottom-up. ~3.6M births/yr in the US. ~30–40% use a parenting app. Subset starting solids and actively managing allergen anxiety estimated at 10–20%. Even at $5 one-time, that's a multi-million dollar addressable pool in the US alone.

## Competitive Landscape

### Direct Competitors
| App | Focus | Price | Gap |

### Indirect Competitors
- Huckleberry (sleep + baby log) — no allergen tracking
- BabyCenter / What to Expect — content + community, no structured tracking
- Ovia Pregnancy/Parenting — broad parenting, not allergen-specific
- Notes apps / spreadsheets — no structure, no reminders, nothing exportable

### Substitutes
- Paper notes handed to pediatrician
- Facebook/Reddit groups for advice
- "Do nothing" — wing it without a plan

### Our Moat
- Narrow focus (the only app that does allergen intro and reaction tracking well at low price)
- Doctor-ready export (actionable output parents can act on immediately)
- Price point (1/16th the cost of Solid Starts for the relevant feature set)
- ASO wedge on "solid starts alternative" and "baby allergen introduction"

## Market Trends

### Tailwinds
- Rising food allergy prevalence (~8% of US children have food allergies; peanut allergy tripled in 20 years)
- LEAP study popularized early allergen introduction — pediatricians now actively recommend it
- Baby-led weaning (BLW) movement has grown significantly — parents more engaged in starting solids intentionally
- Post-pandemic parent anxiety about baby health is elevated
- Parents increasingly distrust data-harvesting apps — privacy-first positioning is growing in appeal
- App Store users willing to pay for niche, high-utility tools (one-time purchase model works for parenting utilities)

### Headwinds
- Solid Starts has brand recognition and a large content library
- Some parents rely on pediatrician guidance alone and may not see the need for an app
- Narrow window of use (allergen intro is a 4–12 month phase) — LTV limited unless app expands

### Technology Shifts
- AI-assisted reaction assessment could become table stakes (not building in MVP, but watch)
- Wearables for baby health monitoring (Owlet) — adjacent but not competitive

## Adoption Lifecycle Position
- **Where We Are**: Innovators / Early Adopters
- **Chasm Status**: Pre-chasm — need to find and serve the allergy-anxious early adopter parent before crossing to mainstream
- **Beachhead Segment**: First-time parents in English-speaking markets with babies 4–6 months old, active in parenting Reddit/Facebook communities, history of anxiety around baby health decisions
