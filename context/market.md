# Market Context

## Market Size
- **TAM**: $2.5B (flashcard/vocabulary builder app market, 2025)
- **SAM**: ~$400M (English vocabulary + professional terminology mobile apps)
- **SOM**: $125K-440K (Year 1, across all niches -- assumes all apps approved)
- **Methodology**: Top-down from verified market research reports (Market.us, Verified Market Research), cross-referenced with RevenueCat education app benchmarks and competitor revenue data.

## Competitive Landscape
- **Direct Competitors**: Anki ($700K/mo revenue, FSRS algorithm, poor UX), WordUp ($4.99/mo, AI-powered, movie clips), Vocabulary.com ($700K/mo, adaptive AI), Brainscape ($19.99/mo, medical focus)
- **Indirect Competitors**: Duolingo ($1B+ bookings, 135M MAU), Quizlet ($139M revenue, 50M MAU), Memrise ($13.3M revenue, declining), Drops ($13/mo)
- **Substitutes**: Free Anki desktop + community decks, Kindle built-in dictionary, word-of-the-day emails, physical flashcards, reading books, TikTok/Tumblr word content
- **Our Moat**: Currently none. FSRS is open-source, UX can be copied, niche content can be replicated. Potential moat: curated exam-mapped content library + community if built.

## Market Trends
- **Tailwinds**: AI integration in education apps, mobile-first learning preference, growing international student population (IELTS), record medical school enrollment (100K+ US students), dark academia cultural trend, spaced repetition gaining mainstream awareness
- **Headwinds**: Market concentration (Duolingo = 67% of language app revenue), free alternatives abundant, education app Day 30 retention = 2-3%, Apple 4.3 enforcement tightening, AI features becoming table stakes
- **Technology Shifts**: AI tutors (Duolingo Max, Quizlet Q-Chat), adaptive learning algorithms, voice/pronunciation features, cross-platform sync

## Adoption Lifecycle Position
- **Where We Are**: Vocabulary apps = Late Majority (mature). Niche-specific SRS apps = Early Adopter (nascent).
- **Chasm Status**: The broad vocabulary app market is post-chasm. VocabX's niche approach is pre-chasm -- needs to find early adopter beachhead.
