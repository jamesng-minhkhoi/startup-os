# Customer Context

## Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)
- **Who**: Pet parent seeking personalized, AI-powered care guidance (not just record-keeping)
- **Demographics/Firmographics**: Millennial/Gen Z (25-38), urban/suburban, smartphone-native, $50K+ household income
- **Behavioral Signals**: Frequently Googles pet behavior/health questions; reads ingredient labels; has visited vet for non-emergency questions; has pet with allergies or sensitivities; shares pet care with partner
- **Budget Authority**: Spends $1,885+/yr on pets (Gen Z avg). $35/yr is <2% of pet spending.

## Hypothesized User Segments

### Segment 1: First-Time Owner
- **Profile**: New to pet ownership (pet < 12 months), high anxiety, lots of questions
- **JTBD**: "Tell me if I'm doing this right — I don't know what's normal"
- **Key needs**: Behavior guidance, milestone tracking, "is this normal?" reassurance
- **AI chat usage**: High frequency, broad questions ("my puppy won't stop biting", "how much should I feed a 3-month kitten")
- **Scanner usage**: Moderate — learning what products to buy
- **Willingness to pay**: High (anxious, willing to pay for peace of mind)

### Segment 2: Busy Owner
- **Profile**: Experienced but time-constrained, 1-3 pets, shares care with partner/family
- **JTBD**: "Give me the answer fast — I don't have time to research"
- **Key needs**: Quick AI answers, product scanning for safety, family sync for shared care
- **AI chat usage**: Moderate frequency, specific questions ("can dogs eat avocado", "my cat is scratching more than usual")
- **Scanner usage**: High — quick safety checks while shopping
- **Willingness to pay**: Moderate (values convenience, but needs to see clear time savings)

### Segment 3: Superuser
- **Profile**: Experienced, detail-oriented, often has pets with health conditions (allergies, chronic illness)
- **JTBD**: "Help me understand my pet's health deeply and catch problems early"
- **Key needs**: Deep health analytics, ingredient analysis, trend detection, detailed records
- **AI chat usage**: Lower frequency but deeper conversations ("my dog's weight has dropped 5% in 2 months, what should I check")
- **Scanner usage**: Very high — ingredient-level analysis for allergens
- **Willingness to pay**: Very high (already spending more on premium food, supplements, frequent vet visits)

## Jobs to Be Done (Post-Pivot)
- **Functional Job**: "Get personalized, trustworthy answers about my pet's behavior and health without a vet visit"
- **Emotional Job**: "Feel confident I'm giving my pet the best care possible"
- **Social Job**: "Be seen as a responsible, informed pet parent"

## Pain Points (HYPOTHESIZED — NOT YET VALIDATED)
1. Google gives generic, often contradictory pet advice — not personalized to my pet's breed/age/conditions
2. Vet visits for non-emergency questions are expensive ($50-150) and time-consuming
3. Can't tell if a product is safe for my pet without reading tiny labels and cross-referencing allergies
4. Don't know if my pet's behavior is normal or a sign of a problem
5. Overwhelmed by conflicting information online about pet nutrition and wellness

## Customer Language (HYPOTHESIZED)
- "Is this normal for my breed?"
- "Can my dog eat this?"
- "Should I be worried about..."
- "Is this ingredient safe for cats with..."
- "My pet is acting weird — what does it mean?"
