# Customer Context

## Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)
- **Who**: Medical students (MS1-MS2) preparing for anatomy, pathology, and USMLE Step 1 exams
- **Demographics/Firmographics**: Age 22-28, US/Canada/UK, enrolled in medical school, high future earning expectations
- **Behavioral Signals**: Currently using Anki with medical decks (Anking, Zanki), complaining about Anki UX on Reddit, searching for "medical terminology app" or "Anki alternative"
- **Budget Authority**: Personal spending, $15-30/mo typical for study tools (already pay for UWorld, Pathoma, Sketchy, etc.)

## Jobs to Be Done
- **Functional Job**: "Help me memorize medical terminology efficiently so I can pass my exams and perform confidently in clinical settings"
- **Emotional Job**: "Reduce my anxiety about the volume of terms I need to learn; make me feel like I'm making progress"
- **Social Job**: "I need to sound competent during rounds and clinical rotations; I want to keep up with my classmates"
