# Customer Context

## Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)
- **Who**: Pet parent seeking personalized, AI-powered care guidance (not just record-keeping)
- **Demographics/Firmographics**: Millennial/Gen Z (25-38), urban/suburban, smartphone-native, $50K+ household income
- **Behavioral Signals**: Frequently Googles pet behavior/health questions; reads ingredient labels; has visited vet for non-emergency questions; has pet with allergies or sensitivities; shares pet care with partner
- **Budget Authority**: Spends $1,885+/yr on pets (Gen Z avg). $35/yr is <2% of pet spending.

## User Segments & Growth Roles (Updated 2026-04-06 — Value Proposition Framework)

### Segment 1: First-Time / Uncertain Pet Owners → ACQUISITION
- **Profile**: New to pet ownership (pet < 12 months), high anxiety, lots of questions
- **JTBD**: "Tell me if I'm doing this right — I don't know what's normal"
- **Growth role**: Acquisition — primary entry point for new users
- **Key needs**: Behavior guidance, milestone tracking, "is this normal?" reassurance
- **Key features**: AI onboarding/guided setup, AI chat (basic Q&A)
- **Value prop**: AI-powered companion that guides them through daily care with simple, reliable, personalized support — reducing confusion and decision fatigue from day one
- **Messaging**: "Care for your pet with confidence, not guesswork"
- **Willingness to pay**: High (anxious, willing to pay for peace of mind)

### Segment 2: Busy & Convenience-Driven Owners → ACTIVATION
- **Profile**: Experienced but time-constrained, 1-3 pets, shares care with partner/family
- **JTBD**: "Give me the answer fast — I don't have time to research"
- **Growth role**: Activation — these users must see value quickly ("aha moment")
- **Key needs**: Quick AI answers, product scanning for safety, family sync for shared care
- **Key features**: Scan (food/document), smart reminders, homepage quick actions
- **Value prop**: Intelligent, low-effort system that automates tasks, reduces manual input, and delivers quick actionable insights — making pet care fit seamlessly into daily life
- **Messaging**: "Less effort, better care"
- **Willingness to pay**: Moderate (values convenience, needs clear time savings)

### Segment 3: Emotion-Driven Pet Lovers → RETENTION (HIGHEST PRIORITY)
- **Profile**: Deeply bonded with pets, treats pets as family, enjoys capturing/revisiting moments
- **JTBD**: "Help me stay connected with my pet and celebrate our bond every day"
- **Growth role**: Retention — THE survival metric. This is the core segment that keeps users coming back
- **Key needs**: Memories, visual presence of pet in daily life, emotional engagement
- **Key features**: Pet photos/memories, widget (pet on screen), daily moments/timeline
- **Value prop**: Companion experience integrating memories, presence, and daily interaction — unlike utility-only apps, enhances the emotional side of pet ownership
- **Messaging**: "Keep your pet close, wherever you are"
- **Willingness to pay**: High (emotional attachment creates strong switching costs)

### Segment 4: Proactive & Improvement-Oriented Owners → ENGAGEMENT/DEPTH
- **Profile**: Experienced, detail-oriented, often has pets with health conditions (allergies, chronic illness)
- **JTBD**: "Help me understand my pet's health deeply and catch problems early"
- **Growth role**: Engagement/depth — deepens usage and long-term value
- **Key needs**: Deep health analytics, ingredient analysis, trend detection, detailed records
- **Key features**: Health tracking & history, insights/recommendations, habit/routine optimization
- **Value prop**: Intelligent insights, trend tracking, and proactive recommendations that enable a more advanced level of care — analyzing data, identifying patterns, guiding toward better routines
- **Messaging**: "Smarter care starts with better insight"
- **Willingness to pay**: Very high (already spending more on premium food, supplements, frequent vet visits)

### Segment 5: Tech-Forward & AI-Curious Users → DIFFERENTIATION/VIRALITY
- **Profile**: Early adopters, curious about AI tools, value intelligent experiences
- **JTBD**: "Show me what AI can actually do for pet care"
- **Growth role**: Differentiation + virality — these users share and evangelize
- **Key needs**: Seamless AI experience, chat + scanning + automation in one system
- **Key features**: AI assistant (advanced, contextual), smart suggestions (auto actions), scan + AI combo, auto pet profile update
- **Value prop**: Cohesive AI-first experience where AI actively assists, learns, and takes action — making the app both useful and engaging
- **Messaging**: "Experience pet care powered by AI"
- **Willingness to pay**: Moderate-high (values innovation, will pay for best-in-class experience)

### Segment 6: Kids & Family Pet Interaction → FUTURE (LOWEST PRIORITY)
- **Profile**: Families with children who enjoy interacting with pets
- **JTBD**: "Make pets part of our family's daily digital life"
- **Growth role**: Future expansion — not a current priority
- **Key needs**: Fun, visual, engaging experience for kids; parent-guided features
- **Value prop**: Safe and enjoyable space where pets become part of a child's daily experience, while parents get care support
- **Messaging**: "Fun for kids, helpful for parents"
- **Willingness to pay**: Low (parents pay, not kids — must prove value to parent first)

### Growth Priority Order
1. **Retention (emotion-driven)** → survival — if users don't come back, nothing else matters
2. **Activation (busy users)** → users must understand value quickly
3. **Acquisition (first-time owners)** → scale after retention/activation are working
