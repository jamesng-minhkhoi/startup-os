# Company Context

## Identity
- **Company Name**: AllergenFirst (working name)
- **One-liner**: A focused baby allergen introduction tracker that helps parents stay organized, reduce anxiety, and produce clear records for their pediatrician.
- **Stage**: Pre-revenue (idea validated, MVP not yet built)
- **Founded**: 2026
- **Location**: Vietnam (solo developer)

## Vision & Mission
- **Vision** (10-year): The trusted safety layer for parents navigating their baby's first foods and food sensitivities.
- **Mission** (what you do today): Help parents introduce allergenic foods with a structured plan, clear reaction logs, and doctor-ready reports — without paying for a bloated subscription.
- **Core Values**:
  - Calm over anxiety (we don't optimize stress, we reduce it)
  - Clarity over completeness (one job done well beats ten jobs done poorly)
  - Privacy by default (no ads, no data selling)
