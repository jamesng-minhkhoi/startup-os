# Product Context

## Product Overview
- **Product Name**: AllergenFirst
- **Category**: Baby health / parenting utility app
- **Platform**: Mobile (iOS primary, Android secondary)
- **Tech Stack**: TBD (solo dev — likely Flutter or React Native for cross-platform, or Swift for iOS-first)

## Problem & Solution
- **Core Problem**: Parents are anxious about introducing the 9 major allergens (peanuts, eggs, dairy, wheat, soy, fish, shellfish, sesame, tree nuts) to their babies. They lack a structured plan, a safe way to log reactions, and a format they can share with a pediatrician.
- **Current Alternatives**:
  - Solid Starts — broad baby food app, expensive ($79.99/yr), not focused on allergen tracking
  - Huckleberry — sleep/baby log focus, no allergen intro support
  - Paper/notes — no structure, nothing to hand to a doctor
  - Generic note-taking apps — no reminders, no reaction categories, no export
- **Our Solution**: A focused allergen introduction app with a 30-day structured plan, symptom-based reaction logging, photo notes, and a one-tap doctor-ready PDF export.
- **Key Differentiator**: Cheaper, more focused than Solid Starts. Built specifically for allergen introduction — not a general baby tracker or recipe app.

## Positioning
- **Market Category**: Baby allergen introduction protocol tracker (new sub-category — not a generic food log)
- **Target Customer**: First-time parents (especially moms) with babies aged 4–8 months who are starting solids and anxious about food allergies
- **Positioning Statement**: "The only allergen tracker with a built-in 30-day introduction protocol — so you're not just logging reactions, you're following a plan."
- **Grand Slam Offer**: "$7.99 one-time. Reviewed by a pediatric allergist. Free checklist included. 30-day money-back guarantee. Your baby's data never leaves your phone."

## Features & Roadmap

### MVP (v1.0)
- 30-day allergen introduction schedule
- Common allergen checklist (9 major allergens)
- Food introduction calendar (log dates, quantities, reactions)
- Reaction log by symptom type (skin, digestion, respiratory)
- Photo notes for skin reactions
- Reminder system (customizable alerts)
- Doctor-ready PDF export
- Safety disclaimers throughout

### Post-MVP (v1.x)
- Multiple child profiles
- Expanded allergen list (beyond top 9)
- Reaction severity tracking over time
- iCloud / encrypted backup

### Not Building (Explicit Cuts)
- AI diagnosis or medical advice
- Recipe database
- Social community or sharing
- Complex nutrition tracking
- General baby tracking (sleep, diapers, feeding)
- Broad parenting content
