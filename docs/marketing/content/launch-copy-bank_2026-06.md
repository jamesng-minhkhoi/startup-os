## 3. LinkedIn (English)

**✅ Recommended — GenZ/health-focused** (matches the confirmed health positioning; leads with the real pain + 3 concrete capabilities):

> we just launched PETIO 🐾
> and we stopped trying to be "another pet app."
> instead, we're focused on one thing: pet health decisions in real life — made fast + personalized.
> because right now it's chaos:
> you google symptoms → get 50 conflicting answers
> you check food → still not sure if it's safe
> you try to "do the right thing" → but it's guesswork
> so we built PETIO.
> what it actually does:
> – AI chat that understands your pet specifically (via pet profiles)
> – health + behavior guidance based on context, not generic answers
> – food scanner that analyzes safety for your pet, not just "nutrition labels"
> basically: less guessing. more "oh ok, I know what to do now."
> pet health shouldn't feel like googling your way through anxiety. it should feel like clarity.
> if you're into AI, consumer health, or pets — would love to connect 🐶🐱
> https://petiogo.com/
> #buildinpublic #startup #ai #pethealth #consumerai

🟡 Alt tones available: plain founder, investor-focused (clearer positioning/traction tone).

🔴 **Hold — aggressive "fundraising announcement" version.** It frames Petio as a scaling "AI-native platform… opening conversations with investors." Pre-revenue with no traction numbers, this oversells and can hurt credibility with the exact investors it targets. Don't post until there are real signals (installs/retention). Kept for reference only.
