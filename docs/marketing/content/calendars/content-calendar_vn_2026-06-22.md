## The strategic call (read this first)

We are iOS-only in an Android-majority market. If we hard-sell the app now, 70% of the people we reach hit a wall (no Play Store). So for these 2 weeks:

1. **Build trust + presence**, don't promote the app. This matches the Facebook playbook ("no Petio mention for 2+ weeks, become a trusted member first").
2. **Capture Android demand** instead of losing it. Every piece of content drives to:
   - **Primary CTA → free web Food Checker**: `petiogo.com/vi/tools/kiem-tra-thuc-an` (works on any phone, no install) — this is the hero link in every bio.
   - **Android users → waitlist**: "Petio đang lên Google Play — để email nhận thông báo ra mắt sớm." (⚠️ **James must stand up this form/landing section before Jun 22** — without it, every Android impression is wasted.)
   - **iOS users → App Store** (secondary, soft — only when someone asks).
3. **Find the TikTok hook now** so we know what works before Play Store launch turns on the install firehose.

> Cadence is deliberately sane for a 3-person team: ~3 TikToks/wk, ~3 IG/wk, daily-light Threads, daily-light FB presence + 2 value posts/wk. Don't overcommit — consistency beats volume.

## Rules for this sprint

- **All copy native Vietnamese** — never translated from English.
- **Value first, app second.** During iOS-only window, the app is barely mentioned. We earn trust now, sell when Play Store is live.
- **Every bio → Food Checker link.** It's the one CTA that works for 100% of the audience (Android + iOS, no install).
- **Collect Android emails** every time it fits naturally. This list is the launch-day fuel.
- **Don't make medical/diagnosis claims.** "Kiểm tra thành phần", not "chẩn đoán bệnh".
