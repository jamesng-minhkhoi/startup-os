# Petio Messaging Guardrails

The canonical "what Petio is / isn't" — so every human and AI draft describes the product correctly and in the right voice. Born from a real failure mode: **AI tools repeatedly mis-described Petio as a photo/video symptom-diagnosis scanner.** It is not. Read this before writing or generating any Petio content.

---

## ⚠️ The correction (the mistake that keeps happening)

| ❌ WRONG (what AI keeps inventing) | ✅ RIGHT (what Petio actually is) |
|---|---|
| "AI Scanner: chụp ảnh/quay video biểu hiện → AI phân tích dấu hiệu lâm sàng" (symptom diagnoser) | **Food/Product Scanner**: scan a product or its ingredient list → AI analyzes ingredients and **scores safety + flags concerns based on that specific pet's profile** |
| "AI chẩn đoán bệnh qua hình ảnh" | **AI Chat**: ask questions with the **specific context of each pet profile** (breed, age, weight, health history) — personalized answers, not generic |
| Generic "AI pet assistant / pet companion" | **AI pet *health* copilot** — focus is health & safety decisions |

**One-liner (canonical)**: *Petio is an AI pet-health copilot — a food/product safety scanner that scores ingredients against your pet's profile, plus an AI chat that answers with your pet's specific context.*

## What Petio IS
- **Two hero features**: (1) Food/Product Safety Scanner (ingredient analysis + safety score per pet profile), (2) Context-aware AI Chat (answers tied to a selected pet profile).
- **Health-focused.** Helps owners make better food/health/daily-care decisions for *their specific pet*.
- Supporting: health tracking (weight, vaccines, deworming, milestones), reminders.
- **Live on iOS**: https://apps.apple.com/vn/app/petio-ai-pet-care-health/id6745301343 · web: https://petiogo.com
- Android (Google Play): **not yet live** → during this window, drive to web Food Checker + waitlist (see launch-state).

## What Petio is NOT (don't claim)
- ❌ Not a symptom/disease diagnoser from photos or video.
- ❌ Not a vet replacement — "không thay bác sĩ thú y", we help you decide *when* to go.
- ❌ No medical/diagnosis claims. Frame as "kiểm tra thành phần / ingredient safety check", "biết khi nào cần đi thú y".

## Positioning note (a sharpening worth a decision)

The launch drafts converged on a **narrower, sharper** position than `context/product.md`'s broad "AI pet care" with 9 features: **"AI pet-health copilot = food safety scanner + context-aware AI chat."** Leading with one concrete use case ("scan pet food → AI safety analysis for your pet") consistently tested as the strongest hook across LinkedIn, builder communities, and Unikorn.

→ **Recommendation**: adopt this as the public-facing lead message even while the app does more. Surfaced for James/Vy to confirm; not yet written into `context/product.md` as the official positioning.
