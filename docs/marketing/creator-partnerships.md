## The program — one step at a time

`Gifted → Trial → Affiliate → Retainer`

- **Gifted** — sent the free lifetime-premium offer, no strings attached.
- **Trial** — they replied interested, so send the trial + affiliate-preview email.
- **Affiliate** — they want in. Issue a referral code, send the activation email. 50% recurring commission on anyone they refer, for that referral's first 12 months.
- **Retainer** — once someone's referrals are bringing in real revenue, James decides case by case whether to offer a fixed monthly retainer/ambassador arrangement.

Don't skip a step — someone tries the app before they get an affiliate code.

**Email vs. DM:** cold DMs from a brand-new/unwarmed IG or TikTok account are unreliable — they can land in "message requests" nobody checks, or get the account flagged before it's built any trust. Until the accounts are warmed up, treat email as the primary outreach channel and DM as a secondary attempt, not the other way around. Most creators in the 5K–50K range don't have a public email though — see the Contacts sheet's Email column for who actually has one.

## Ground rules

**Instagram DMs**
- Main account: 5 a day, max. Secondary account: 10–12 a day, max.
- One DM per person, ever. If you follow up, do it once, and only after two weeks.
- Don't send the exact same message twice in a row — switch up the opening line each time.
- If either account gets a warning or gets rate-limited, stop and tell James right away.

**Fastlane posts**
- Aim for 2–3 IG Reels and 1–2 TikToks a week, spread out rather than all on one day.
- Since Fastlane's content is AI-generated, use the platform's disclosure label for it.

**Reddit/Facebook (creator-adjacent, not the main content-tracker flow)**
- Answer the question for real first. Only bring up Petio if it's genuinely the right answer to what they asked. Never copy the same reply into another thread.
