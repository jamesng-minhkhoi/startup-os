## What Petio Is

**AI pet care that knows your pet personally.**

Not a generic chatbot. Not a health tracker. An AI that has your pet's breed, age, allergies, and health history — so every answer and every product scan is specific to *your* pet.

**Category**: Personal AI for pet care  
**Tagline**: Your pet's personal AI  
**App Store subtitle**: The AI that already knows your pet

## Who We're Building For

### Primary — Allergy Dog Parent (US beachhead)
- Dog has confirmed food allergy or sensitivity
- Manually reads ingredient labels before every purchase
- Has had at least one allergic reaction scare ($200–2,000 ER visit)
- Spends $60–150/mo on specialty food
- 25–45 years old, US-based, skews female
- Hangs out in Facebook allergy groups, r/dogallergies, breed-specific subreddits
- **Why they pay**: One wrong treat = $500 ER bill. $5.99/mo is insurance.

### Secondary — Anxious First-Time Owner
- New pet, 0–12 months in, overwhelmed by conflicting Google advice
- Asks 3–10 questions per week: "Is this normal?" "How much should they eat?"
- **Why they pay**: Replaces $50–150 vet visits for non-emergency questions

### Secondary — Behavior Decoder
- Pet has ongoing behavioral issue (anxiety, aggression, house training)
- Already spent money on generic advice or trainers with no results
- Wants to understand *why*, not just get a tip
- **Why they pay**: Trainers cost $100–200/session. Petio is fraction of the cost.

### Vietnam — Worried Millennial Pet Parent (HCMC/Hanoi)
- 25–34, first independent pet owner (not a family pet)
- Confused by conflicting advice online, distrusts Vietnamese brand quality
- Active in Facebook pet groups, buys on Shopee
- **Why they pay**: 79K VND/mo is less than one bag of premium food

## Our Unfair Advantage

The combination nobody else has:

1. **AI with full pet context** — breed, age, allergies, health history in every answer. ChatGPT has none of this.
2. **Scanner personalized to your pet's allergies** — not just "contains chicken," but "Max is allergic to chicken — unsafe."
3. **Scanner + chat in one flow** — scan a product, ask the AI why it's flagged, in the same conversation.
4. **Behavior guidance as a core feature** — not an afterthought. Personalized to your pet's actual situation.
