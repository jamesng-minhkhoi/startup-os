# Vietnam Distribution Partnerships
**Last updated**: 2026-04-12 | **Status**: Pending Vy's decision

---

## Why This Exists

We have 3 people and $0 marketing budget. The Vietnam 10K target requires TikTok, Facebook groups, KOL outreach, Zalo — none of us can do that full-time. Instead of hiring (no cash) or giving equity (permanent and expensive), we build an affiliate partner network.

**Recommendation: Revenue share. No equity for distribution partners.**

---

## The Model

| Tier | Who | What they do | What they get |
|------|-----|-------------|---------------|
| **Affiliate** | Anyone with a referral link | Shares link in their network | 20% of subscription revenue from their referrals, recurring 12 months |
| **Ambassador** | TikTok/Facebook content creators | Creates content + posts regularly | 25% of revenue + "Petio Ambassador" badge + featured on site |
| **Distribution Partner** | Group admins (10K+ members) or KOLs | Brings existing audience, co-creates campaigns | 30% of revenue first 6 months, then 20% ongoing |

### The Math

```
Per paying user (Vietnam):
  Subscription:         79,000 VND/mo
  Apple/Google cut:    -23,700
  LLM costs:           -12,000
  ─────────────────────────────
  Gross margin:         43,300 VND/mo

Revenue share:
  Affiliate (20%):      15,800 VND/mo → partner earns
  Petio keeps:          27,500 VND/mo

  Distribution (30%):   23,700 VND/mo → partner earns
  Petio keeps:          19,600 VND/mo
```

**Key: we pay nothing until users convert to paid. Zero risk. Fully aligned incentives.**

Example: a partner brings 100 paying users → they earn ~1.6M VND/month passively.

## Red Lines

1. **No equity for marketing partners** — revenue share only, period.
2. **No upfront payments** — earn when they deliver paying users.
3. **No exclusive partnerships** — we want 10+ affiliates, not 1 dependent.
4. **No medical claims** — "trợ lý AI" (AI assistant), never "bác sĩ AI" (AI doctor).
5. **No spam** — partners who spam groups get cut. We need those communities for the long term.
