# Petio — Go-to-Market Strategy
**Last updated**: 2026-04-12 | **Markets**: US/Global (primary) + Vietnam (parallel)

---

## GTM Motion: Product-Led Growth

No sales. No community-first. The scanner is the PLG entry point:
- **Time-to-value: under 30 seconds** — scan barcode → see allergen flags
- **Natural paywall** — "You've used your 3 free scans. Unlock unlimited."
- **Output is shareable** — scan result cards drive organic acquisition

---

## Beachhead: US Allergy Dog Parent

~6M US households have a dog with food allergies. ~2.4M actively seek solutions.  
Year 1 target: 5,000–15,000 users → 400–1,200 paying → $29K–$86K ARR.

This is a validation-scale beachhead — enough to prove retention, WTP, and unit economics before expanding.

## Channel Strategy
