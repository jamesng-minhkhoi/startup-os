## What Petio Is

**Category**: Personal AI for pet care  
**One-liner**: Your pet's personal AI.  
**Positioning statement**: For pet parents who worry about food safety, behavior, or "is this normal?" — Petio is the only pet care AI personalized to their specific pet, combining a context-aware chatbot with a product safety scanner.

**The real competition is not another app.** It's Google + gut feeling + occasional vet visit. That is the status quo for 90% of pet parents.

## Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)

**Primary beachhead: The Allergy Dog Parent**

| | |
|---|---|
| **Pet** | Dog with confirmed food allergy or sensitivity |
| **Behavior** | Reads labels manually, has had at least one allergic reaction, actively seeks product safety info |
| **Spend** | $60–150/mo on specialty food |
| **Demographics** | 25–45, US-based, skews female 65/35 |
| **Pain** | Anxious, trial-and-error feeding, distrusts generic advice, wants certainty |
| **Channels** | Facebook allergy groups, r/dogallergies, breed subs |
| **Why they pay** | One allergic reaction = $200–2,000 ER visit. $5.99/mo is insurance. |

**Secondary segments:**
- **Anxious New Parent** — first-time owner, drowning in contradictory Google advice, 3–10 AI questions/week
- **Behavior Decoder** — pet has behavioral issue, has already spent $500+ on trainers, wants to understand the *why*

## Competitive Battlecards

**vs. ChatGPT**
- Their pitch: "Ask me anything — for free."
- Our counter: "ChatGPT doesn't know your dog has a chicken allergy. Petio does. ChatGPT can't scan a product at PetSmart. Petio can."
- "But it's free" objection: "You spend $100/mo on specialty food. One wrong treat = $500 vet bill. $5.99/mo for certainty is insurance."

**vs. Hapu (best scanner)**
- Their pitch: "Scan pet food. Know what's inside."
- Our counter: "Hapu tells you what's in the food. Petio tells you if it's safe for YOUR dog."

**vs. DongoPet (closest all-in-one)**
- Their pitch: "Everything your pet needs in one app."
- Our counter: "DongoPet gives you a little of everything. Petio gives you the one thing that matters: an AI that knows your pet and catches what you'd miss."

**vs. PawHub (Vietnam)**
- Their pitch: "All-in-one pet platform" (directory + service listings)
- Our counter: "PawHub helps you find a vet. Petio helps you not need one."
- Positioning: PawHub = Yelp for pets. Petio = AI doctor for pets.

## What Petio Is NOT

- Not a replacement for veterinary care (always recommend vet for medical emergencies)
- Not a generic chatbot (if the answer isn't personalized, the product isn't working)
- Not a health tracking app (tracking supports the AI, it's not the value prop)
- Not a pet social network
