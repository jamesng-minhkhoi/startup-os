# Petio Strategy
**Last updated**: 2026-04-12

---

## What We Are

**Petio is the AI pet care assistant that knows your pet personally.**

Not a generic chatbot. Not a health tracker. An AI that has your pet's breed, age, allergies, and health history — so every answer and every product scan is specific to your pet, not pets in general.

**Category we're creating**: Personal AI for pet care  
**One-liner**: AI pet care that knows your pet personally  
**Tagline**: Your pet's personal AI

---

## Who We're For

**Primary target: Dog owner with a pet that has food allergies (US beachhead)**

They manually read ingredient labels. They've had at least one allergic reaction scare. They spend $60–150/mo on specialty food. They want certainty, not opinions. One wrong treat = $200–2,000 ER visit. Petio at $5.99/mo is insurance.

**Secondary:**
- First-time pet owner overwhelmed by contradictory Google advice
- Pet owner whose pet has a behavioral issue and has already wasted money on generic advice

**Vietnam beachhead**: Millennial pet parent in HCMC — first independent pet owner, confused by conflicting advice, worried about food safety, active in Facebook pet groups.

## What We Are NOT Doing

- **No vet telemedicine** — we guide, we don't diagnose
- **No pet social network or feed** — pure utility
- **No marketplace or e-commerce** — ops trap
- **No hardware / GPS tracking** — different product entirely
- **No service directory** — that's PawHub's play, not ours
- **No smart reminders** — removed, zero engagement in v0
