**Date:** April 7, 2026
**For:** James, Ngoc, Vy — all 3 co-founders should align on this before any outreach
**Context:** $0 marketing budget. Need distribution partners for Vietnam 10K user target.

---

## Why Partnerships (Not Solo Execution)

We have 3 people. The Vietnam growth playbook requires:
- 5 TikTok videos/week
- Daily Facebook group engagement
- Blog content bi-weekly
- Zalo OA management
- Giveaway campaigns
- KOL outreach

That's a full-time marketing job. None of us are doing marketing full-time. Either we hire (no cash), bring on a marketing co-founder (equity — expensive), or build a partner network (revenue share — cheap, scalable).

**Recommendation: Partner network with revenue share. No equity for distribution.**

---

## Partnership Model: Revenue Share Affiliate

### The Deal

| Tier | Who | What They Do | What They Get |
|------|-----|-------------|---------------|
| **Affiliate** | Anyone with a referral link | Shares link in their network | 20% of subscription revenue from their referrals, recurring 12 months |
| **Ambassador** | Content creators (TikTok/Facebook) | Creates content + posts regularly | 25% of revenue + "Petio Ambassador" badge + featured on site |
| **Distribution Partner** | Group admin (10K+ members) or KOL | Brings existing audience, co-creates campaigns | 30% of revenue for first 6 months, then 20% ongoing |

### The Math Everyone Should See

```
Per paying user (Vietnam):
  Subscription:        79,000 VND/mo
  Apple/Google cut:   -23,700
  LLM costs:          -12,000
  ────────────────────────────
  Gross margin:        43,300 VND/mo

Revenue share breakdown:
  Affiliate (20%):     15,800 VND/mo → partner gets
  Petio keeps:         27,500 VND/mo

  Ambassador (25%):    19,750 VND/mo → partner gets
  Petio keeps:         23,550 VND/mo

  Distribution (30%):  23,700 VND/mo → partner gets
  Petio keeps:         19,600 VND/mo
```

### At Scale

| Scenario | Paying Users | Monthly Revenue | Partner Share (20% avg) | Petio Keeps |
|----------|-------------|----------------|------------------------|-------------|
| Month 3 | 500 | 39.5M VND ($1,580) | 7.9M VND ($316) | 31.6M VND ($1,264) |
| Month 6 | 2,000 | 158M VND ($6,320) | 31.6M VND ($1,264) | 126.4M VND ($5,056) |
| Month 12 | 5,000 | 395M VND ($15,800) | 79M VND ($3,160) | 316M VND ($12,640) |

**Key: We pay nothing until users convert to paid.** Zero risk. Fully aligned incentives.

### Why NOT Equity

| | Revenue Share | Equity |
|--|--------------|--------|
| Cost at 500 paying users | 7.9M VND/mo ($316) | 5% of company (worth $250K+ at Series A) |
| If partner stops working | They stop earning | They still own 5% forever |
| Reversible? | Yes — end agreement | No — equity is permanent |
| Vietnamese partners expect? | Yes — affiliate models are normal (Shopee, TikTok Shop) | No — equity is confusing for most marketing partners |
| Scales to multiple partners? | Yes — unlimited affiliates | No — equity pie runs out fast |

**Only consider equity if someone is joining as a 4th co-founder with full-time commitment + 4-year vesting.** For distribution/marketing partners, always revenue share.

## Decision Framework for Vy + Ngoc

### What Vy Should Consider

Vy is Product/Marketing. This partnership strategy directly affects her domain.

**Questions for Vy:**
1. **Do you want to own partner management?** — someone needs to be the point person for affiliates. Track their performance, pay out monthly, maintain relationships. This is a PM/marketing role.
2. **Content quality control** — partners will create content about Petio. Who reviews it? Vy should set brand guidelines: what to say, what NOT to say (no medical claims, no "thay thế bác sĩ thú y").
3. **Vietnamese voice** — Vy is native Vietnamese. The outreach templates above need her review. Does the tone sound right? Is the slang natural? She should adjust before posting.

**Recommendation for Vy:** Own the partner program. This is high-leverage — you manage 5-10 partners who each do the work of one person. Your job shifts from "create all content" to "manage a content network."

### What Ngoc Should Consider

Ngoc is Designer. Partnership affects her through:

**Questions for Ngoc:**
1. **Brand assets for partners** — partners will need: app screenshots (Vietnamese), logo, color codes, approved images, maybe a simple "Petio Partner Kit" (1 page PDF with brand assets + dos/don'ts)
2. **App Store Vietnamese screenshots** — this is a blocker. Partners will drive traffic to stores. If the listing looks English-only, Vietnamese users bounce.
3. **Social media templates** — can Ngoc create 3-5 Canva templates that partners can customize? (TikTok thumbnail, Facebook post graphic, Instagram story) This ensures brand consistency without Ngoc designing every post.

**Recommendation for Ngoc:** Create a simple partner kit (1 day of work) that enables partners to create on-brand content without needing design approval each time.

### What James Should Consider

**Questions for James:**
1. **Referral tracking system** — you need to build this before onboarding any partners. Start simple: unique UTM links per partner + spreadsheet. Upgrade to in-app referral codes when you have 5+ active partners.
2. **RevenueCat attribution** — can you tag subscribers by referral source? This is critical for calculating payouts.
3. **Partner agreement** — even for rev share, put it in writing. Simple 1-page agreement: "Partner refers users via [link]. Petio pays [X]% of net subscription revenue monthly. Either party can terminate with 30 days notice."

**Recommendation for James:** Build the referral tracking first (even if it's just UTM links). Don't recruit partners until you can track who brought what.

## Red Lines — What We Won't Do

1. **No equity for marketing partners.** Revenue share only. Period.
2. **No upfront payments** until we see results. Affiliate model means they earn when they deliver.
3. **No exclusive partnerships.** We want 10+ affiliates, not 1 dependent partner.
4. **No medical claims in partner content.** "Trợ lý AI" (AI assistant), never "bác sĩ AI" (AI doctor).
5. **No spam.** Partners who spam groups get cut immediately — this burns our reputation in communities we need.
