# Distribution Partnership Strategy — NEEDS VY'S INPUT

## Context

James identified an opportunity: use revenue share affiliate partnerships to solve our $0 marketing budget problem for Vietnam. Vietnamese founders commonly post in Facebook networking groups (e.g. FoundersUp - Đời Làm Chủ) to find marketing partners. This could accelerate the 10K Vietnam user target without spending cash or diluting equity.

## The Proposal

**Model:** Revenue share affiliate (20-30% of subscription revenue per referred paying user, recurring 12 months). No equity. No upfront cash.

### 3 Tiers

| Tier | Who | They Get |
|------|-----|----------|
| Affiliate | Anyone with a referral link | 20% of revenue from their referrals |
| Ambassador | Content creators (TikTok/Facebook) | 25% + featured on site |
| Distribution Partner | Group admin 10K+ members / KOL | 30% for 6 months, then 20% |

### The Math

```
Per paying user (79K VND/mo subscription):
  After store cut + LLM costs = 43,300 VND/mo gross margin
  20% to affiliate = 15,800 VND/mo
  Petio keeps = 27,500 VND/mo

At 100 paying users from a partner:
  Partner earns: ~1.6M VND/mo passively
  Petio keeps: ~2.75M VND/mo
```

### Why Revenue Share, Not Equity

Revenue share at 2,000 paying users costs ~$1,264/month.
5% equity at a $10M valuation = $500,000.
Revenue share is 30-100x cheaper and fully reversible.
