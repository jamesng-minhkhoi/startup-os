## Content Rules

1. **Every post has a scanner or AI CTA** — not generic "download Petio" but specific: "Quét thức ăn của boss ngay" or "Hỏi AI Petio về triệu chứng này"
2. **Vietnamese-first, not translated** — write natively in Vietnamese, don't translate English posts. Use Vietnamese slang ("boss" for pets, "sen" for pet parents)
3. **Cite Vietnamese sources** — local vet clinics, Vietnamese pet food brands, VN pricing. Not American sources.
4. **2000+ words, personal voice** — same quality bar as English blog. No thin AI-generated content.
5. **Local brands in comparisons** — Me-O, SmartHeart, Ganador, Classic Pets, Pedigree VN, Royal Canin VN. Not Purina/Blue Buffalo.
6. **Seasonal/cultural hooks** — Tet, rainy season, summer heatstroke. Vietnamese calendar, not Western.

## Distribution

1. **SEO** — target Vietnamese keywords with near-zero competition
2. **Facebook groups** — share in breed-specific and city-specific VN pet groups
3. **Zalo OA** — push new posts to followers
4. **TikTok** — short clips from each post with scanner demos
