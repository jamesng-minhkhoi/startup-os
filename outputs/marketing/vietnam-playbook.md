**Last updated**: 2026-04-12 | **Target**: 10,000 users by July 7, 2026 | **Budget**: <$1,000

## Vietnam vs. US — Key Differences

| Factor | Vietnam | US/Global |
|--------|---------|-----------|
| Discovery | Facebook groups + word-of-mouth | Google Search + App Store |
| Messaging | Zalo (86.6%, 74M users) | iMessage/WhatsApp |
| Mobile OS | Android 70%+ | iOS 55%+ |
| Influencer cost | Micro-KOL: ~$150/post | Micro: $500–2,000/post |
| Trust signals | Community recommendations > brand ads | Reviews + ratings |
| Content style | Cute, anime-style, Vietnamese models | Lifestyle photography |
| Engagement peak | 7:00–9:00 PM | Varies |

**Key insight**: Facebook groups are the App Store in Vietnam. Discovery happens through group recommendations, not search.

---

## Vietnam ICP

**Worried Millennial Pet Parent (HCMC/Hanoi)**
- 25–34, middle class+, smartphone-native
- Often first independent pet owner (not a family pet)
- Googles "chó ăn được gì," buys pet food on Shopee/PetMart
- Confused by conflicting advice, worried about food safety (VN brands have trust issues)
- Spends 200–500K VND/mo on pet food + supplies
- Currently asks Facebook groups or trusts pet shop sellers

## Phase 3: TikTok Ignition (Weeks 5–8) → 2,500 users

TikTok is the 10K user unlock. Without it, ceiling is ~3–5K users.

### 4 Content Formats (in Vietnamese)

| Format | Hook | Example |
|--------|------|---------|
| **"Boss đang ăn gì?"** | Scan a popular VN brand on camera → show result | "Tôi quét Ganador cho Golden Retriever của mình... và đây là kết quả" |
| **"Sen Việt hay mắc sai lầm này"** | Common VN feeding mistakes (cơm thừa, xương gà, nước mắm) | "3 thứ người Việt hay cho chó ăn mà không biết là nguy hiểm" |
| **"So sánh thức ăn cho chó"** | Side-by-side scan of cheap vs premium brands | "Pedigree vs Ganador — Petio chấm điểm thế nào?" |
| **"Hỏi AI về boss"** | Ask Petio a Vietnamese-specific question → show personalized answer | "Tôi hỏi AI tại sao chó tôi cứ gãi tai..." |

**Cadence**: 5 posts/week. No ad spend for first 4 weeks — test organic reach first.
