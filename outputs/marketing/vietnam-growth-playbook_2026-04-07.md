**Target:** 10,000 Vietnamese users by July 7, 2026
**Budget:** Under $1,000 total (bootstrapped)
**Team:** James (primary executor) — playbook designed so anyone can follow it

---

## Vietnam Market Reality Check

This is NOT the US market. Everything below is specific to Vietnam.

| Factor | Vietnam | US/Global |
|--------|---------|-----------|
| **Dominant platform** | Facebook (92% of internet users, 72M users) | Instagram/TikTok |
| **Messaging app** | Zalo (86.6%, 74M users) — NOT WhatsApp, NOT iMessage | iMessage/WhatsApp |
| **Video** | TikTok (80.9%) — fastest growing, esp Gen Z | Same |
| **Mobile OS** | Android 70%+ | iOS 55%+ |
| **Discovery** | Facebook groups + word-of-mouth + Zalo | Google Search + App Store |
| **Trust** | Community recommendations > brand ads | Reviews + ratings |
| **Engagement peak** | 7:00 PM — 9:00 PM | Varies |
| **Content style** | Cute, anime-style, Vietnamese models convert better | Lifestyle photography |
| **Influencer cost** | Micro-KOL: ~$150/post (3-10M VND) | Micro: $500-2,000/post |
| **Giveaway culture** | Minigames, lucky wheels, comment contests — HUGE in Vietnam | Less effective |
| **Price sensitivity** | Very high — free tools win, freemium converts slowly | Moderate |

**Key insight:** In Vietnam, **Facebook groups are the App Store**. People discover apps through group recommendations, not search. Your #1 acquisition channel is Facebook group presence, not ASO.

### Phase 2: Seeding (Week 3-4) — Goal: 500 users

Now you start posting — but NOT promoting Petio yet. You're building credibility first.

#### Facebook Group Strategy

**The 3-Week Trust Sequence:**

**Week 1 (already done):** Observe only. Note top questions.

**Week 2-3: Be Helpful (NO Petio mentions)**

Post 2-3 times per week in each group. Content types that work in Vietnamese pet groups:

| Post Type | Example | Why It Works |
|-----------|---------|-------------|
| **Answer questions** | Someone asks "Chó ăn nho được không?" → Write a detailed, helpful answer with sources | Builds reputation as knowledgeable |
| **Share your blog posts** | "Mình vừa viết bài chi tiết về thức ăn an toàn cho chó, hy vọng giúp ích cho các sen" → link to blog | Value-first, no product mention |
| **Share food checker tool** | "Mình tìm được tool kiểm tra thức ăn miễn phí, khá tiện" → link to /vi/tools/food-checker | Free tool, not an app push |
| **Personal story** | "Boss mình bị dị ứng thức ăn, đây là cách mình phát hiện ra..." | Emotional connection, builds trust |

**Week 4: Soft Introduction**

After 2 weeks of being helpful, naturally mention Petio:
- "Mình xây app này cho boss của mình, bây giờ muốn chia sẻ cho mọi người. Quét thức ăn xem có an toàn cho boss không. Miễn phí nha."
- ALWAYS lead with "miễn phí" (free). Vietnamese users are very price-sensitive.
- Include a screenshot or short video of the scanner working
- Ask for feedback, not downloads: "Mọi người thử xem có hữu ích không?"

### Phase 3: Ignition (Week 5-8) — Goal: 2,500 users

This is where growth accelerates. TikTok should be getting traction. Facebook groups know who you are.

#### Minigame / Giveaway Campaign

**This is HUGE in Vietnam.** Minigames and giveaways drive massive engagement that doesn't work in the US.

**Campaign: "Quét & Trúng — Scan to Win"**

| Detail | |
|--------|---|
| **Mechanic** | 1. Download Petio<br>2. Create a pet profile<br>3. Scan any pet food product<br>4. Screenshot the scan result<br>5. Post screenshot in the Petio Facebook group/page with hashtag #PetioQuetTrung<br>6. Tag 2 friends who have pets |
| **Prizes** | 1st: 500K VND (Royal Canin gift pack)<br>2nd-5th: 200K VND Shopee voucher<br>6th-20th: 1 month Petio Plus free |
| **Budget** | ~1.5M VND total (~$60) |
| **Duration** | 2 weeks |
| **Why it works** | Forces app download + profile creation + first scan (activation). Tagging friends = organic reach. Screenshot sharing = social proof. In Vietnam, even small prizes drive massive participation. |

**Post the giveaway in all 10 Facebook groups** (by now you have 3 weeks of credibility).

**Promote on TikTok:** Make a video about the giveaway. "Quét thức ăn boss → trúng Royal Canin miễn phí. Link trong bio."

#### Micro-Influencer Outreach (5-10 pet TikTokers)

**Vietnamese pet KOL rates: ~3-5M VND ($120-200) per TikTok video.**

| Step | Detail |
|------|--------|
| **Find creators** | Search #ThuCung, #ChoMeo on TikTok VN. Look for 10K-100K followers, high engagement (comments > likes ratio). StarNgage lists 22.9K Vietnamese pet creators |
| **Outreach template** | "Chào bạn! Mình là James, developer của Petio — app quét thức ăn thú cưng bằng AI. Mình muốn hợp tác với bạn 1 video TikTok review app. Budget: [X] VND. Bạn quét thức ăn boss trên camera, chia sẻ trải nghiệm thật. Không cần script — cứ thoải mái. Quan tâm không?" |
| **Content brief** | 1. Show Petio scanning a pet food brand<br>2. React naturally to the results<br>3. Mention it's free<br>4. Tag @petio.vn<br>5. That's it — don't overscript |
| **Budget** | 5 creators × 4M VND avg = 20M VND (~$800) |
| **Target** | 5 videos, each reaching 50-100K views = 250-500K total reach |

**Start with 2-3 nano creators (1-5K followers, 500K-1M VND each)** to test before spending on bigger ones.

## Vietnam-Specific Hacks

### 1. "Thử thách quét thức ăn" (Food Scan Challenge)
Create a TikTok challenge: film yourself scanning your pet's food with Petio. Use sound "Oh no" or "Ủa sao lại vậy?" for reaction. Tag #PetioChallenge. This format is native to Vietnamese TikTok culture.

### 2. Cross-post to Shopee reviews
When users scan a pet food product, prompt them: "Bạn mua sản phẩm này trên Shopee? Để lại review giúp các sen khác!" — drives word-of-mouth in the e-commerce ecosystem where 52% of VN pet parents buy.

### 3. Zalo Mini App (Future)
Zalo supports Mini Apps (like WeChat Mini Programs). A food checker Zalo Mini App would give you direct access to 74M Zalo users without requiring an app download. Consider this for Phase 2 (after proving PMF).

### 4. Tet / Holiday Campaigns
Vietnamese holidays drive huge engagement. Plan themed content:
- **Tet (Lunar New Year)**: "Boss ăn gì trong mâm cỗ Tết?" — scan common Tet foods
- **Valentine's (14/2)**: "Chocolate cho boss? ĐỪNG!" — educational + emotional
- **Trung Thu (Mid-Autumn)**: "Bánh trung thu có an toàn cho boss?"

### 5. Facebook Livestream
Vietnamese users love Facebook Live. Do a weekly 15-min livestream: "Hỏi AI Petio bất cứ gì về boss" — take questions from viewers, answer live using Petio. This builds trust and demonstrates the product in real-time.

### 6. User-Generated Content Loop
After users scan and get results, prompt: "Chia sẻ kết quả lên Facebook để cảnh báo các sen khác!" — natural UGC that drives awareness in their network.

### 7. "Sen của tháng" (Pet Parent of the Month)
Feature the most active user each month on your Facebook page. Vietnamese users LOVE recognition. This drives engagement and loyalty.

## What Can Go Wrong + Mitigation

| Risk | Probability | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|------------|--------|-----------|
| TikTok doesn't hit | Medium | HIGH — kills the 10K target | Redirect budget to Facebook group promotions + more giveaways. Settle for 5K users. |
| Google Play delays | Low | HIGH — 70% of VN users locked out | Submit ASAP. Have APK sideload link as backup |
| Facebook group admins remove posts | Medium | Medium | Never hard-sell. Always lead with value. Have 10 groups so losing 2-3 is fine |
| PawHub copies scanner content | Low | Low | They'd need to build a scanner product, not just content |
| Low retention after install | Medium | HIGH — users churn | Focus on onboarding: force first scan within 60 seconds of profile creation |
| Giveaway attracts low-quality users | Medium | Medium | Require profile creation + first scan to enter. Not just "follow and comment" |
