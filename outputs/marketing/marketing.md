## Content That Works

**US — "Is This Safe?" format (TikTok/Reels)**
1. Pick a popular, trusted brand (Milk-Bones, Beggin' Strips, Blue Buffalo)
2. Scan with Petio on camera
3. Show the result — especially if something is flagged
4. No voiceover needed. Let the result speak.

**Vietnam — "Boss đang ăn gì?" format**
1. Popular VN brand (Ganador, Me-O, SmartHeart, Pedigree VN)
2. Scan on camera, in Vietnamese
3. Community seeding: post scan results in FB groups as helpful info, not as an ad

**What NOT to create:**
- Generic "10 tips for pet parents" listicles
- Founder story / meet the team content
- Pet photo contests
- Medical advice content that could create liability
