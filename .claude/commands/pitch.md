# Pitch Builder Workflow

Build an investor pitch narrative and deck outline. Combines positioning, financial story, and persuasion frameworks.

## Steps

1. **Load Context**: Read all context files. A pitch needs everything.

2. **Narrative Architecture** (skill: `storybrand-messaging`, `made-to-stick`):
   - Build the pitch story arc: Problem → Insight → Solution → Traction → Ask.
   - Apply StoryBrand: customer is the hero, you are the guide.
   - Apply Made to Stick: is the core message Simple, Unexpected, Concrete, Credible, Emotional, Story-driven?

3. **Positioning Clarity** (skill: `obviously-awesome`):
   - What market category are you claiming?
   - What are the competitive alternatives and why are you different?
   - Can you articulate positioning in one sentence?

4. **Market Sizing** (skill: `competitive-brief`):
   - TAM/SAM/SOM with methodology.
   - Bottom-up market sizing (preferred over top-down).

5. **Offer & Value** (skill: `hundred-million-offers`):
   - Apply the Value Equation to show why the product is a no-brainer.
   - What makes this a Grand Slam Offer?

6. **Persuasion Layer** (skill: `influence-psychology`, `marketing-psychology`):
   - Apply relevant mental models: social proof, scarcity, anchoring, loss aversion.
   - Ethical application only — never misrepresent.

7. **Deck Outline**:
   - Generate a 10-12 slide outline with key message per slide:
     1. Title / One-liner
     2. Problem
     3. Solution
     4. Market size
     5. Product / Demo
     6. Business model
     7. Traction
     8. Competition
     9. Team
     10. Financials
     11. Ask
     12. Appendix

8. **Output**: Save to `outputs/strategy/pitch_{date}.md`.

## Arguments

$ARGUMENTS — Stage (pre-seed, seed, series A), ask amount, and any specific investor concerns to address.
