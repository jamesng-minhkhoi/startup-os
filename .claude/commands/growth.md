# Growth Audit Workflow

Audit your growth engine: metrics, conversion funnels, channel performance, and retention. Identifies the #1 bottleneck and prescribes fixes.

## Steps

1. **Load Context**: Read `context/metrics.md`. What are the current KPIs and how are they trending?

2. **Metrics Review** (skill: `metrics-review`, `north-star-metric`):
   - What is the North Star Metric? Is it the right one?
   - Review the metric tree: acquisition → activation → retention → revenue → referral.
   - Identify which stage of the funnel is the bottleneck (Theory of Constraints from marketing-psychology).

3. **CRO Audit** (skill: `cro-methodology`, `page-cro`, `signup-flow-cro`, `onboarding-cro`, `paywall-upgrade-cro`):
   - Audit the relevant conversion points based on where the bottleneck is.
   - Apply the appropriate CRO skill for each conversion point.
   - Score each conversion point 0-10.

4. **Channel Evaluation** (skill: `marketing-ideas`, `paid-ads`, `seo-audit`, `content-strategy`, `social-content`):
   - Which channels are driving growth? Apply the Pareto Principle (80/20).
   - Is the channel mix right for this stage?
   - Identify one channel to double down on and one to cut.

5. **Retention Analysis** (skill: `improve-retention`, `hooked-ux`, `churn-prevention`):
   - What does the retention curve look like?
   - Apply the Hooked model: Trigger → Action → Variable Reward → Investment.
   - Identify churn causes and prevention strategies.

6. **Growth Loops** (skill: `growth-loops`, `referral-program`):
   - Are there flywheel effects? Can users bring more users?
   - Design or optimize a referral mechanism.

7. **Output**: Save to `outputs/marketing/growth-audit_{date}.md` with scored bottleneck analysis and prioritized fixes. Update `context/metrics.md`.
