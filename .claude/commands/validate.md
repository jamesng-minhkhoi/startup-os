# Idea Validation Workflow

Validate a startup or product idea using Lean Startup + Mom Test methodology. Designed to kill bad ideas fast and strengthen good ones.

## Steps

1. **Articulate the Idea**: Clearly state what the product is, who it's for, and what problem it solves. If the user is vague, push for specificity.

2. **Mom Test Assessment** (skill: `mom-test`):
   - Has the user talked to potential customers? Score quality of conversations 0-10.
   - Are they getting facts and commitments, or compliments and opinions?
   - Design 5-7 Mom Test questions that could falsify the idea.
   - Identify the scariest question they haven't asked yet.

3. **Lean Canvas** (skill: `startup-canvas`, `lean-startup`):
   - Fill in the Startup Canvas (preferred over Lean Canvas for its defensibility test).
   - Apply the Can't/Won't defensibility test.
   - Identify the riskiest assumption in each section.
   - Score the overall canvas 0-10.

4. **Value Assessment** (skill: `hundred-million-offers`):
   - Apply the Value Equation: (Dream Outcome x Perceived Likelihood) / (Time Delay x Effort & Sacrifice).
   - Can this become a Grand Slam Offer? What's missing?
   - Score the offer 0-10.

5. **Market Check** (skill: `blue-ocean-strategy`, `crossing-the-chasm`):
   - Is this a red ocean (competing on existing terms) or blue ocean (creating new demand)?
   - Where does this sit on the technology adoption lifecycle?
   - Who is the beachhead segment?

6. **Verdict**: Give a clear GO / ITERATE / KILL recommendation with specific reasoning. No hedge. Be honest.

7. **Output**: Save to `outputs/strategy/validation_{idea-name}_{date}.md`. Update `context/product.md`.

## Arguments

$ARGUMENTS — The idea to validate. Can be a one-liner or a detailed description.
